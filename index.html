More than what we wear

Culture is not
a trend. It's a story.

Tradition Threads began with a simple idea: students should be able to find traditional clothing for school events, cultural celebrations, festivals, and special occasions without feeling overwhelmed by price or choice.

We want to make it easier to explore attire from different backgrounds, learn the stories behind traditions, and celebrate each other with curiosity and respect. The project aims to compare suppliers, listen to student preferences, and build a collection that reflects the community.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Some details about this project prototype.

Are the products and prices real? +

No. The current catalogue uses sample products and prices for demonstration. The project team needs to verify supplier details, availability, sizes, and final prices before accepting orders.

Can I place a real order on this website? +

Not yet. The bag calculates a sample subtotal and can prepare an order enquiry email. It does not reserve stock, accept payments, or store orders in a database.

How do I ask about sizing or availability? +

Add an item to your bag and choose “Prepare order enquiry,” or use the feedback form to prepare a message. The team must configure a real contact email before launch.

Why is this website a prototype? +

The project charter includes researching student needs and suppliers, comparing prices, preparing a catalogue, promoting the service, and reviewing feedback. This site demonstrates the online browsing experience while those operational pieces are confirmed.
